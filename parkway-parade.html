Is it necessary for me to sign up for a plan?
  • There is no need for a plan to receive chiropractic care from us. Some patients come in for "pay per visit" adjustments. However, a single adjustment provides only temporary relief and does not address the root cause, so your pain will return.
  • With a proper plan, you save money AND receive our holistic care: adjustments, dry needling, shockwave therapy, customised exercises and re-examinations, all at a lower cost.
  • It's unlikely your problem happened in an instant. It's usually from habits over a long period, so it's unrealistic to expect it to vanish in one or even a few visits.
Will chiropractic adjustments cause me pain?
  • Certain adjustments may cause slight discomfort initially, depending on the joint or condition, but you should not feel pain after an adjustment.
  • After your first adjustment, slight discomfort lasting about 12 to 24 hours is usual.
What is the number of adjustments that I need?

A consultation is needed to determine the number of adjustments for each person. Discussing your short- and long-term health goals with us helps us create the best care plan for you. Everyone is different, so there's no "one size fits all".
